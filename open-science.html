Research principles

Open science & reproducibility

Making approved research outputs discoverable, reproducible, and appropriately attributed.

Research documentation

Each project aims to identify data provenance, analysis code, version history, methods, and contributor roles.

Where outputs live

GitHub hosts the website and links to code. Approved data may be deposited in an institutional or disciplinary repository. Citable software releases may be archived with a DOI.

Attribution

Contributions are described by role and linked to supporting work when possible. Git history supplements, but does not replace, team agreements about authorship and credit.

Release policy

Only publicly releasable materials belong on this website. Check DOE project, sponsor, institution, collaborator, and data management requirements before publication.
